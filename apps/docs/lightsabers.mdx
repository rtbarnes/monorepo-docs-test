# Lightsabers

The lightsaber is the signature weapon of the Jedi Order and their Sith counterparts. It consists of a plasma blade powered by a kyber crystal, emitted from a metal hilt.

## Components

A lightsaber is built from several key parts:

- **Kyber crystal** — The Force-attuned crystal that focuses the blade's energy and determines its color.
- **Hilt** — The metal handle that houses the crystal, power cell, and emitter.
- **Emitter** — The opening from which the plasma blade is projected.
- **Activation switch** — The control used to ignite or deactivate the blade.

## Blade colors

The color of a lightsaber blade is determined by the wielder's connection to their kyber crystal.

| Color | Common wielder |
| ----- | -------------- |
| Blue | Jedi Guardians |
| Green | Jedi Consulars |
| Purple | Mace Windu |
| Yellow | Jedi Sentinels and Temple Guards |
| Red | Sith (synthetic, "bleeding" the crystal) |
| White | Ahsoka Tano (purified crystals) |

## Combat forms

Jedi and Sith train in seven classical forms of lightsaber combat:

1. **Form I: Shii-Cho** — The foundational form.
2. **Form II: Makashi** — A precise dueling form.
3. **Form III: Soresu** — A defensive form focused on blaster deflection.
4. **Form IV: Ataru** — An acrobatic, aggressive form.
5. **Form V: Shien / Djem So** — A counterattacking form.
6. **Form VI: Niman** — A balanced, diplomatic form.
7. **Form VII: Juyo / Vaapad** — An aggressive form channeling inner darkness.

## Notable lightsabers

- **Anakin Skywalker's lightsaber** — Later wielded by Luke Skywalker and Rey.
- **The Darksaber** — A unique black-bladed lightsaber tied to Mandalorian leadership.
- **Darth Maul's double-bladed saber** — A staff-style weapon used in Sith combat.
