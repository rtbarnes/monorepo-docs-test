# Millennium Falcon

The Millennium Falcon is a modified YT-1300 light freighter, famous for its speed, its smuggling exploits, and its pivotal role in the Galactic Civil War.

## Specifications

- **Class:** YT-1300f light freighter
- **Manufacturer:** Corellian Engineering Corporation
- **Length:** 34.75 meters
- **Hyperdrive:** Modified Class 0.5
- **Crew:** 2 (minimum), with room for passengers and cargo

## Notable owners

- **Lando Calrissian** — won the ship in a game of sabacc before losing it to Han Solo.
- **Han Solo and Chewbacca** — used the Falcon for smuggling runs and Rebellion missions.
- **Rey** — piloted the ship during the war against the First Order.

## Famous feats

- Completed the Kessel Run in less than twelve parsecs.
- Helped destroy both the first and second Death Stars.
- Outran Imperial Star Destroyers through the asteroid field near Hoth.

## Modifications

- Upgraded sublight engines for unmatched speed.
- Reinforced hull plating and military-grade deflector shields.
- Quad laser cannons mounted on the dorsal and ventral hulls.
