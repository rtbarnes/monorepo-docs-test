# Tatooine

Tatooine is a remote desert planet in the Outer Rim Territories, orbiting twin suns. It plays a pivotal role across the Skywalker saga as the homeworld of Anakin and the boyhood home of Luke.

## Key facts

- **Region:** Outer Rim Territories
- **Suns:** Two (binary system)
- **Terrain:** Desert, canyons, salt flats
- **Capital:** Bestine
- **Native species:** Jawas, Tusken Raiders

## Notable locations

- **Mos Eisley** — A spaceport town, famously described as a "wretched hive of scum and villainy."
- **Mos Espa** — Site of the Boonta Eve Classic podrace.
- **Jundland Wastes** — Rugged territory inhabited by Tusken Raiders.
- **Lars Homestead** — The moisture farm where Luke Skywalker grew up.

## Cultural significance

Tatooine sits at the crossroads of smuggling routes and Hutt influence, which is why characters like Jabba the Hutt operate there with little interference from the galactic government. Its harsh climate shapes a self-reliant population of moisture farmers, traders, and outlaws.
