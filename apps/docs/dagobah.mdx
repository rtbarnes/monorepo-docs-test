# Dagobah

Dagobah is a murky, swamp-covered world in the Sluis sector of the Outer Rim. Cut off from galactic civilization, it served as the hiding place of Jedi Master Yoda during the reign of the Empire.

## Geography

- Dense bogs, fog-shrouded jungles, and twisted gnarltrees cover the surface.
- No settlements, spaceports, or technology exist on the planet.
- A strong connection to the Force permeates the wilderness, including a dark side cave near Yoda's hut.

## Inhabitants

- **Yoda** — the exiled Grand Master of the Jedi Order.
- **Swamp slugs** — massive worm-like creatures lurking in the bogs.
- **Bogwings, sleen, and dragonsnakes** — native fauna adapted to the wet terrain.

## Notable events

- Yoda's self-imposed exile after the rise of the Galactic Empire.
- Luke Skywalker's Jedi training under Yoda following the Battle of Hoth.
- Luke's vision in the dark side cave, foreshadowing his confrontation with Vader.
