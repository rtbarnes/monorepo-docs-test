# Lightsabers

A lightsaber is a distinctive energy weapon wielded by Jedi and Sith alike. Its blade of pure plasma is shaped and contained by a kyber crystal at the heart of the hilt.

## Construction

- **Hilt** — a hand-machined metal handle, often custom-built by its wielder.
- **Kyber crystal** — a Force-attuned crystal that focuses the blade and determines its color.
- **Power cell** — provides the energy required to ignite and sustain the plasma blade.
- **Emitter** — channels and shapes the blade as it leaves the hilt.

## Blade colors

- **Blue** — traditionally carried by Jedi Guardians.
- **Green** — favored by Jedi Consulars such as Luke Skywalker and Yoda.
- **Red** — wielded by the Sith, who bleed kyber crystals to corrupt them.
- **Purple** — famously carried by Mace Windu.
- **Yellow** — used by Jedi Temple Guards and Sentinels.

## Notable wielders

- **Obi-Wan Kenobi** — master of Form III, Soresu, the defensive form.
- **Darth Vader** — a fearsome duelist whose red blade became a symbol of the Empire.
- **Ahsoka Tano** — known for her dual white-bladed sabers after leaving the Jedi Order.

## Combat forms

The Jedi codified seven classical forms of lightsaber combat, each suited to different temperaments and tactical situations, ranging from the foundational Shii-Cho to the aggressive Juyo.
