# Hoth

Hoth is a frozen world in the Outer Rim Territories, famous as the location of Echo Base, the secret headquarters of the Rebel Alliance during the Galactic Civil War.

## Geography

- A perpetual ice age covers the surface in glaciers and snowfields.
- Subzero temperatures plunge further at night, making unsheltered survival nearly impossible.
- Meteor impacts and caverns shape the rugged, frozen terrain.

## Inhabitants

- **Tauntauns** — reptomammals ridden by Rebel patrols across the snow plains.
- **Wampas** — towering predators that lurk in ice caves and hunt warm-blooded prey.
- No permanent sentient population calls Hoth home.

## Notable events

- The Rebel Alliance establishes Echo Base after evacuating Yavin 4.
- Imperial probe droids discover the base, triggering the Battle of Hoth.
- Luke Skywalker survives a wampa attack and receives a vision of Obi-Wan Kenobi guiding him to Dagobah.
