# Tatooine

Tatooine is a remote desert planet in the Outer Rim Territories, orbiting twin suns and best known as the homeworld of Anakin and Luke Skywalker.

## Geography

- Two suns scorch the surface, creating vast deserts and dune seas.
- Settlements such as Mos Eisley and Mos Espa serve as trade hubs.
- The Dune Sea and Jundland Wastes dominate the landscape.

## Inhabitants

- **Humans** — moisture farmers and traders who eke out a living from the arid climate.
- **Jawas** — diminutive scavengers who travel the dunes in massive sandcrawlers.
- **Tusken Raiders** — nomadic warriors native to the deep desert.

## Notable events

- The discovery of a young Anakin Skywalker by Qui-Gon Jinn.
- Luke Skywalker's departure to begin his Jedi training.
- The fall of Jabba the Hutt at the Great Pit of Carkoon.
