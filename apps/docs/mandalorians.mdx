# Mandalorians

Mandalorians are a clan-based people from the planet Mandalore, known across the
galaxy for their distinctive armor, martial culture, and complex history of
conflict with the Jedi Order.

## Culture

Mandalorian identity is rooted in *Resol'nare*, the Six Actions, which bind
members of the culture regardless of species or origin:

- Wear your armor.
- Speak the language (Mando'a).
- Defend yourself and your family.
- Raise your children as Mandalorians.
- Contribute to your clan's welfare.
- Answer the call of the Mand'alor when summoned.

## Beskar armor

Mandalorian armor is traditionally forged from *beskar*, a rare iron alloy
mined on Mandalore. Beskar can deflect blaster bolts and withstand a lightsaber
strike, making it one of the most prized materials in the galaxy.

## Notable Mandalorians

- **Jango Fett** — bounty hunter and genetic template for the Republic's clone army.
- **Boba Fett** — Jango's unaltered clone "son" and a legendary bounty hunter.
- **Din Djarin** — a Child of the Watch who protected Grogu.
- **Bo-Katan Kryze** — leader who sought to reunify the clans and wield the Darksaber.

## The Darksaber

The Darksaber is a unique black-bladed lightsaber created by Tarre Vizsla, the
first Mandalorian inducted into the Jedi Order. Possession of the blade is
traditionally tied to leadership of Mandalore.
