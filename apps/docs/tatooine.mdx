## Overview

Tatooine is a harsh desert world orbiting twin suns in the galaxy's Outer Rim Territories. Despite its remote location and inhospitable climate, the planet plays a pivotal role in galactic history as the homeworld of both Anakin and Luke Skywalker.

## Geography

The surface is dominated by vast seas of sand, rocky mesas, and canyons carved by ancient winds. Daytime temperatures can exceed survivable limits, driving most settlements underground or into shaded enclaves.

## Inhabitants

- **Humans** — Moisture farmers eke out a living by harvesting water vapor from the atmosphere.
- **Jawas** — Diminutive scavengers who travel the dunes in massive sandcrawlers.
- **Tusken Raiders** — Nomadic warriors, also known as Sand People, fiercely protective of their territory.
- **Hutts** — Crime lords who run their empires from palaces deep in the wastes.

## Cultural significance

Tatooine sits at the crossroads of smuggling routes, making it a haven for bounty hunters, mercenaries, and fugitives. Its dual sunsets have become one of the most iconic images in the Star Wars saga.
