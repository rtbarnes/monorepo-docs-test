# Lightsabers

A lightsaber is an energy weapon wielded by Jedi, Sith, and other Force-sensitive warriors. Each blade is unique to its builder, reflecting their training, philosophy, and connection to the Force.

## Construction

- **Kyber crystal** — a Force-attuned crystal that focuses the blade and determines its color.
- **Hilt** — the metal housing that contains the power cell, emitter, and activation controls.
- **Emitter matrix** — shapes the plasma blade and contains it in a stable arc.

## Blade colors

- **Blue** — traditionally wielded by Jedi Guardians focused on combat.
- **Green** — favored by Jedi Consulars who emphasize diplomacy and Force mastery.
- **Red** — synthetic crystals "bled" by the Sith through anger and pain.
- **Purple** — rare, famously carried by Mace Windu.
- **Yellow** — wielded by Jedi Temple Guards and Sentinels.

## Notable wielders

- **Yoda** — carried a short green-bladed saber suited to his acrobatic Form IV style.
- **Darth Vader** — wielded a red blade forged after his fall to the dark side.
- **Ahsoka Tano** — dual-wielded white sabers after purifying corrupted kyber crystals.
