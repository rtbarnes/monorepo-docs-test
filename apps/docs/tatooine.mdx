# Tatooine

Tatooine is a remote desert planet in the Outer Rim Territories of the Star Wars galaxy. Orbiting twin suns, it is best known as the homeworld of Anakin and Luke Skywalker.

## Geography

Tatooine's surface is dominated by sand dunes, rocky canyons, and salt flats. Surface temperatures climb high enough that most activity happens at dawn and dusk.

- **Suns:** Tatoo I and Tatoo II
- **Region:** Outer Rim
- **Terrain:** Desert, canyons, mesas

## Inhabitants

You'll find a mix of moisture farmers, smugglers, and indigenous species:

- **Jawas** — scavengers who travel in sandcrawlers and trade salvaged droids.
- **Tusken Raiders** — nomadic warriors native to the deep desert.
- **Hutts** — crime lords who run much of the planet's economy from Mos Espa and beyond.

## Notable locations

- **Mos Eisley** — a spaceport famous for its cantina and lax law enforcement.
- **Mos Espa** — a podracing hub and former home of Anakin Skywalker.
- **Jundland Wastes** — rugged badlands frequented by Tusken Raiders.

## Why it matters

Tatooine appears across multiple Star Wars films and series, serving as both a starting point and a recurring backdrop for the Skywalker saga.
